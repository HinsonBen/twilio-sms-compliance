How SMS Consent Is Obtained

The authorized recipient's mobile number is explicitly configured as an SMS recipient for the operational alert application. The recipient authorizes the application to send operational desk alerts to that number before the number is enabled for messaging.

What Messages Are Sent

Messages are limited to operational desk alerts and notifications generated by the application. SMS is not used to place orders or conduct purchases.

Message Frequency

Message frequency is variable and depends on whether an applicable operational alert occurs.
