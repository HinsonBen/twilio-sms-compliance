SMS Program

These Terms & Conditions govern the SMS messaging program provided by Trader CoPilot. The program delivers operational desk alerts generated by the Trader CoPilot application to one authorized recipient: the Trader CoPilot account owner/operator.

SMS is not used for marketing, customer solicitation, order placement, or transactions. Trades or orders cannot be placed by text message.

Authorization and Consent

The account owner/operator authorizes SMS by configuring their personal mobile number as the authorized SMS recipient in the Trader CoPilot application. By configuring that number, the account owner/operator agrees to receive Trader CoPilot operational desk alerts at that number. Trader CoPilot sends SMS only to the configured authorized recipient. See SMS Authorization for details.

Message Frequency and Fees

Message frequency varies. Messages are sent only when an operational alert is triggered. Message and data rates may apply. Message delivery depends on your mobile carrier's network availability.

Opting Out (STOP)

Reply STOP to any message to opt out. You will receive a one-time confirmation, and no further messages will be sent to that number. You may also remove the number as the authorized SMS recipient in the Trader CoPilot application. To resume messages, reply START from the authorized number while it is still configured as the authorized recipient.

Help (HELP)

Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or email hinsonben@gmail.com.
